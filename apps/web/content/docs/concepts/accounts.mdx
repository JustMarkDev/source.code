Everything on source.code belongs to an **account**. An account is either a **person** or an **organization**, and both live in one namespace: `source.code/<address>`. A name can never mean two things.

| Detail  | Person                 | Organization                        |
| ------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Has     | A full name            | A name (a company, team or project) |
| Owns    | Their own repositories | Its repositories, whoever made them |
| Made by | Signing up             | A signed-in person, afterwards      |
| Members | Just them              | People, each with a role            |

## One login, any account

You sign in once, as yourself. That login can act as your own account and as every organization you belong to, one at a time. Nobody needs a second login, a second password or a second token, and switching is instant.

- **On the web**, the account menu in the header lists them. Pick one and the dashboard, the new repository form and the settings follow it. `⌘K` has "Switch to ..." too.
- **In the CLI**, `sx account switch northwind`, or `-A northwind` for a single command. See [Accounts in the CLI](/docs/cli/authentication#accounts).

## Creating an organization

Any signed-in person can create one from the account menu ("New organization"). The creator becomes its first **owner**, and the organization, not the person, owns what is created in it. If the creator leaves, the repositories stay.

## Roles

| Role   | Can do                                                                                     |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Owner  | Everything: roles, the address, inviting owners. An organization always has at least one.  |
| Admin  | Edit the organization, invite people, remove members. Cannot touch owners or other admins. |
| Member | Work in its repositories. Cannot create repositories or manage people.                     |

An organization always needs an owner. The last owner cannot be demoted, removed or leave: make someone else an owner first, then step down. That is also how you hand an organization over.

## Addresses

A new account gets a generated address, like `quiet-otter-482`. It says nothing about who or what the account is, so nobody can sign up as someone else's name or a company's. People see names, not addresses: a repository shows its owner's full name or the organization's name, and the address appears small on the profile with a button to copy it.

You can change it in settings. The old address keeps working: links to it redirect to the new one, and nobody else can take it. Taking back your own old address ends its redirect.

A few names are never available, because they are pages or belong to us: `admin`, `settings`, `organizations`, `new`, `docs` and similar.
