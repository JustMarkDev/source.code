# Code of conduct

This project is for building a good forge. Be useful and be decent to the
people you work with, in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other space
that belongs to the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the code or the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep feedback specific and kind. Say what to change and why.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, or using the project's spaces for advertising unrelated to it.
- Anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests,
and block people who break these rules, without warning for serious cases.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, @JustMarkDev, using the email on
their GitHub profile. Reports are kept private.

This applies to the project's spaces, and to the public behavior of someone
speaking for the project elsewhere.
